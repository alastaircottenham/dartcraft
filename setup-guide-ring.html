Dartboard Height & Throw Line

Before mounting your dartboard, ensure it is positioned at the correct regulation height. These measurements apply to standard steel-tip dartboards.

MeasurementValue
Bullseye height173 cm from the floor
Throw distance237 cm from face of the board
⚠️
The bullseye must be exactly 173 cm from the floor. The throw line (oche) must be at least 237 cm from the face of the board — measure from the front surface of the board, not the wall.

LED Ring Setup

The Autodarts ring provides integrated LED lighting and holds the three cameras. The following steps cover full ring assembly, LED strip installation, and camera leg assembly.

2.1 Ring Assembly

The ring is made up of 9 outer segments and a set of inner ring segments. Follow the steps below to assemble the full ring and fit the inner ring.

1
Gather the 9 main ring segments. Connect them firmly together in groups, pressing each joint fully until seated, to form 3 separate sub-sections.
The 9 ring segments connected into 3 sub-sections
Press each joint firmly until fully seated
Three completed sub-sections
2
Using the 3 leg-mount segments, connect one between each sub-section to complete the full ring. Ensure all joints are fully clicked into place before proceeding.
Connecting a leg-mount segment between sub-sections
Completed full ring
3
Place one inner ring segment at each joint around the inside of the main ring. The inner ring strengthens the overall structure and prevents light from the LEDs bleeding through the outer joints.

At the three positions where the camera legs will be mounted, use the inner ring leg segments with a hole. You will need:
  • 6× Inner Ring Segment (plain)
  • 3× Inner Ring Segment Leg — Left
  • 3× Inner Ring Segment Leg — Right
Inner ring segment types: plain (6×), left leg (3×), right leg (3×)
Fitting inner ring segments around the inside of the ring
4
Once all the inner ring pieces are in place, secure each with the included M3 screws.
Secure each inner ring segment with the included M3 screws

2.2 LED Strip Installation

The LED strip runs the full circumference of the ring and is routed through one of the leg-mount pieces to bring the power connector to the outside.

1
Wipe down the inner wall of the ring with isopropyl alcohol and allow it to dry completely. This ensures a strong, lasting bond.
2
Fully unwind the LED strip from its holder. From the outside of the ring, feed the strip through the larger hole in one of the leg-mount pieces, leaving just the power connector visible on the outside.
Threading the LED strip through the leg-mount hole
Start the strip at the end closest to the power connector
3
Starting at the end closest to the power connector, peel back a short section of the adhesive backing and press the strip firmly into the inner wall groove. Continue peeling and pressing, a section at a time, working your way around the ring until the strip is fully installed.
Peel and press a section at a time around the ring
LED strip fully installed
💡
Tip: For extra bonding strength, reinforce the strip with 3M double-sided tape (1 cm width) along the groove before application.

2.3 Ring Legs Assembly

Each leg consists of an upper section with the camera already pre-installed and a lower section. Assemble the first two standard legs before moving on to the LED power leg.

1
For each of the first two camera legs: feed the camera USB cable down through the lower leg, then insert the two hinge pins on the upper leg into the lower leg and press firmly together until secure. Repeat for the second leg.
Feed the camera USB cable down through the lower leg first
2
Attach the two assembled camera legs to the ring using the included M4 screws, tightening them securely.
Attach leg to ring with M4 screws
Tighten screws securely
3
For the third leg at the LED strip power connector:
  1. Feed the LED power supply cable through the lower leg first
  2. Feed the camera USB cable through in the same way
  3. Press the upper and lower leg together
  4. Connect the LED strip connector to the LED power supply cable
  5. Attach this leg to the ring using the included M4 screws
Feed LED power cable through lower leg
Connect the LED strip to the power cable
Attach LED leg to ring
Tighten with M4 screws

2.4 Camera Positioning

There are two valid camera positioning options for Autodarts. Choose one and apply it consistently — your camera legs must align with either dartboard segment 6 or segment 11 on the horizontal axis.

Option A — Camera on Segment 6

One horizontal camera leg aligns with segment 6 on the right side of the board.

Option B — Camera on Segment 11

One horizontal camera leg aligns with segment 11 on the left side of the board.

Camera positioning options — segment 6 (right) or segment 11 (left)

2.5 Wall Installation

Use the supplied two-part installation template to accurately mark the three leg mounting hole positions on the wall. The template must be positioned from the exact bullseye centre — remove the dartboard first if already mounted.

ℹ️
The template has two parts: the Bullseye Part (positions at the board centre) and the Camera Leg Part (marks hole positions for each leg).
Template parts: Bullseye part (left) and Camera Leg part (right)

Join the two template parts — they should snap together. Use a drop of glue or tape if needed.

Join the two parts — they snap together. Tip: use a drop of glue or adhesive tape if they don't clip firmly.

Option A — Camera on Segment 6

1
Draw a vertical and horizontal line through the bullseye using a spirit level. Place the template corner at the bullseye centre, aligned with those lines. Mark the 1st camera leg holes and draw a green reference line.
Step 1 — Mark 1st leg and draw green reference line
2
Rotate the template clockwise, align it to the centre and the newly drawn green line. The horizontal line should pass through the corners of the template hole. Mark the 2nd camera leg holes and draw a blue line.
Step 2 — Mark 2nd leg and draw blue reference line
3
Rotate the template clockwise again, align it with the centre and the newly drawn blue line. Check the horizontal line. Mark the 3rd camera leg holes.
Step 3 — Mark 3rd leg position

Option B — Camera on Segment 11

1
Same starting position as Option A. Mark the 1st camera leg holes and draw a green reference line.
Step 1 — Mark 1st leg and draw green reference line
2
Rotate the template counter-clockwise, align it to the centre and the newly drawn green line. The horizontal line should pass through the corners of the template hole. Mark the 2nd camera leg holes and draw a blue line.
Step 2 — Mark 2nd leg and draw blue reference line
3
Rotate the template counter-clockwise again, align it with the centre and the newly drawn blue line. Check the horizontal line. Mark the 3rd camera leg holes.
Step 3 — Mark 3rd leg position

2.6 Alternative: Position Using Surround

If you have a standard surround with an outer diameter of 67.5 cm, the ring legs are designed to just touch it. Simply place the ring around the surround and mark the mounting holes.

⚠️
Important: You still need to confirm the horizontal camera position aligns with segment 6 or 11.
Positioning the ring using a standard 67.5 cm surround — check horizontal camera aligns with segment 6 or 11

2.8 Minimum Backing Board Size

If mounting on a backing board, the minimum size to accommodate the ring with legs is 80 × 76 cm.

2.9 Screw Covers

Each leg includes two snap-on caps (marked I and II) to cover the mounting screws. Match the markings on the caps to the leg, insert the pins into the holes, and press in. Use a screwdriver in the side slot to remove them.

⚠️
Use screws with a half-round head, max thread diameter ø4.5 mm. Oversized heads will prevent the caps from seating properly.
Screw cover caps — markings I and II must match the leg. Insert pins into holes and press in. Use a screwdriver in the side slot to remove.
